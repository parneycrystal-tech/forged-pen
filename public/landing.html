The experience

Forged Pen is more than a tool. It's a place you can go to get lost in your story, like the dim corner of a beautiful old library, with a window that looks out into a green garden. The world outside agrees to wait. Your story is right where you left it, however long you've been away. Agnes kept the record. Finn has been thinking about your last chapter.

Finn
Your writing coach
Finn reads everything before he says a word. He asks the question that unlocks the thing you've been avoiding. He never writes your prose. He never says "just push through." He coaches you to find the story yourself.
Agnes
The record keeper
Agnes works quietly. She reads every chapter, keeps the record of your story, notices when your characters are moving in a direction your notes haven't caught up to yet. Meticulous. Slightly pointed. But when she does speak, she's usually right.
Who this is for

Built for writers whose brains work differently, however you get the words down.

You have ADHD, autism, or another neurodivergent experience that shapes how you create. Executive dysfunction, rejection sensitive dysphoria, perfectionism paralysis. No other writing tool was designed for any of it.

You plan. You outline before you draft, and you want a tool that respects the structure you've already built instead of fighting it or ignoring it. Agnes keeps your Story Bible as ground truth, and flags anything that drifts from your own plan.

You discover the story by writing it. You have fragments that feel alive and homeless, scenes before you know where they belong. Embers is where those fragments live until Agnes finds them a home.

You've been skeptical of AI writing tools because you don't want a ghostwriter. You want to grow as a writer, not outsource the work. Finn refuses to write for you. That refusal is the product.

Successful high-volume authors have developmental editors, research assistants, and coaches on call. Most writers write alone. This is that support, without needing money or connections to get it.

What we believe.

Every word on the page should belong to the writer. We will never write it for you. Finn is a coach, not a ghostwriter. That line does not move.

Struggling to start is not laziness, and your brain's way of working is not a problem to fix. Forged Pen is built around it. No word count goals. No streaks. No guilt about time away.

Human creativity is irreplaceable. AI's proper role is to support it, not supplant it. The voice, the instinct, the lived experience that makes writing matter: that's yours. Always.
